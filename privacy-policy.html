Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026  ·  Effective immediately

Summary: GroundsNearMe collects only the minimum personal information needed to provide our cricket ground booking service. We do not sell your data. We do not run advertising networks. Data is stored securely in Pakistan-adjacent infrastructure.

1. Who We Are

GroundsNearMe ("we", "us", "our") operates the website groundsnearme.pk — a cricket ground discovery and slot-booking platform serving Karachi, Pakistan.

Contact: hello@groundsnearme.pk  |  WhatsApp: +92 336 2308445

2. What Personal Data We Collect

We collect only data that is strictly necessary to provide our service:

When you create an account

  • Full name — to identify your booking at the venue
  • Pakistan mobile number (e.g. 0300-XXXXXXX) — the ground owner contacts you via WhatsApp to confirm your booking
  • Email address (optional) — for account recovery only

When you make a booking

  • Ground name, date, time slot, and booking reference (GNM-YYYY-XXXXXX)
  • Your name and phone number — shared with the ground owner to complete the booking
  • Payment status (unpaid / paid) — we do not collect card numbers or banking details. Payment is made directly at the ground in cash.

Automatically collected data

  • Booking and preferences data is stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device. We also write confirmed bookings to our secure cloud database (Supabase).
  • We do not use cookies for advertising or behavioural tracking.
  • We do not use Google Analytics, Facebook Pixel, or any third-party ad network.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To confirm and display your bookings in "My Bookings"
  • To share your contact details with the specific ground owner for the ground you booked
  • To generate unique booking reference codes
  • To allow you to cancel bookings if needed

We will never use your data for unsolicited marketing without your explicit opt-in consent.

4. Third-Party Services

We use a small number of trusted third-party services to power this website:

  • Supabase (supabase.co) — cloud database where booking records and ground information are stored. Data is encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Cloudflare (cloudflare.com) — website hosting and image delivery (CDN). Cloudflare Privacy Policy
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — typography. Google may log your IP address when fonts are loaded. Google Privacy Policy
  • Phosphor Icons (unpkg.com CDN) — icon library. No personal data is collected by this CDN beyond standard server logs.
  • WhatsApp — when you click "Contact Owner" or "Join Game", you are directed to WhatsApp. WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies from that point.

5. Data Storage & Security

  • All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS.
  • Database access is protected by Row Level Security (RLS) — ground owners can only access bookings for their own venues.
  • We store the minimum data required and do not create behavioural profiles.
  • Your account session data is stored in your browser's localStorage. Clearing your browser data will log you out.

6. Data Retention

  • Booking records are retained for 24 months from the booking date for operational and dispute resolution purposes.
  • Account data is retained while your account is active. You may request deletion at any time.
  • Browser localStorage data is deleted when you clear your browser storage or log out.

7. Your Rights

Under Pakistan's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) 2023 and PECA 2016, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction — ask us to correct inaccurate data
  • Deletion — request that we delete your account and associated data
  • Objection — object to processing of your data

To exercise these rights, email us at hello@groundsnearme.pk or WhatsApp us on +92 336 2308445. We will respond within 14 working days.

8. Children's Privacy

GroundsNearMe is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us immediately and we will delete the data.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
