Fair Play & Protection

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026  ·  Compliant with Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014

Our Fair Play Guarantee: At GroundsNearMe, cricket belongs to the players. We ensure clear cancellation terms, zero surprise fees, and full protection when weather or facility issues prevent play.

1. Overview of Booking Model

GroundsNearMe operates primarily on a Pay-at-Ground model. In most cases, players reserve slots online and pay the venue owner upon arrival in cash or via digital wallet (JazzCash, Easypaisa, Raast, or direct bank transfer).

Where advance tokens or payments are agreed directly with ground management, this policy governs cancellation rights, refunds, and rescheduling obligations for both players and ground owners.

3. Weather & Rain Cancellation Policy

Karachi weather, monsoon downpours, or sudden waterlogging can render outdoor and tape-ball pitches unplayable. In such events:

  • If the ground manager declares the pitch unplayable prior to start of play, no fees are due, and any advance paid is 100% refundable or transferable to another available date of the team captain's choice.
  • If rain stops play mid-match before 50% of the booking time has elapsed, the venue owner shall offer a pro-rata discount or a replacement slot for the unplayed duration.
  • For covered indoor venues, rain policy does not apply unless access roads to the facility are impassable or venue operations are flooded.

4. How to Cancel a Booking

You can cancel your booking through two quick and accessible channels:

  1. In-App via Website: Go to My Bookings, find the upcoming booking card, click Cancel Booking, and confirm. The slot is released instantly back into the public directory.
  2. Via GroundsNearMe WhatsApp: Send your Booking Reference (e.g. GNM-1002) to +92 336 2308445. Our support team operates daily from 9:00 AM to 11:00 PM PKT.

5. Refund Processing Timelines

Where advance refunds are approved:

  • Digital Wallets (JazzCash / Easypaisa / Raast): Refunded within 2 to 24 hours of confirmation.
  • Direct Bank Transfers: Processed within 1 to 3 Pakistani banking days.
  • Cash Payments at Ground: Settled on the spot directly with venue management.

6. Dispute Resolution & Player Advocacy

If a ground owner refuses a legitimate refund for weather, double-booking, or timely cancellation under these terms:

  1. Notify GroundsNearMe Support via WhatsApp (+92 336 2308445) or email hello@groundsnearme.pk with your Booking Reference and summary of the issue.
  2. Our team will mediate directly with venue management within 24 hours.
  3. Venues with repeat verified refund violations will be delisted from GroundsNearMe and forfeit their Pro badges to protect the community.

7. Statutory Rights under Pakistani Law

Nothing in this policy limits your rights under applicable consumer protection legislation, including the Sindh Consumer Protection Act 2014 and the Electronic Transactions Ordinance 2002.
