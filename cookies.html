Cookies & Storage Policy

Last updated: 10 September 2026

Short version: GroundsNearMe does not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels. We use browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your session and bookings on your own device. We load fonts from Google, which may log your IP address.

1. Do We Use Cookies?

GroundsNearMe does not set traditional HTTP cookies for advertising, behavioural tracking, or analytics. We do not use:

  • Google Analytics
  • Facebook Pixel or any Meta tracking
  • Any advertising network cookies
  • Session cookies that track you across websites

2. Browser localStorage (Essential Storage)

We use your browser's localStorage — a type of storage that lives entirely on your device and is not transmitted to third parties. This is how we store your account session and bookings locally so you stay logged in between visits.

Key What it stores When it expires
gnm_user Your account name and phone number (player session) When you log out or clear browser storage
gnm_registered_users Locally registered user profiles on this device When you clear browser storage
gnm_bookings Your booking history (local copy — also synced to server) When you clear browser storage
gnm_cookie_consent Records whether you have accepted this policy 365 days, or when you clear browser storage

This storage is classified as strictly necessary to provide the service. Clearing it will log you out and remove your local booking history (your bookings are also backed up in our database).

3. Third-Party Requests

When you visit GroundsNearMe, your browser makes the following requests to third-party services. These are needed for the site to look and function correctly:

  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) — loads the typography used on the site. Google's servers may log your IP address and browser type as part of standard web server logs. We have no control over this. Google Privacy Policy →
  • Unpkg CDN (unpkg.com) — loads the Phosphor icon library. Standard server access logs may be created. No personal data is processed beyond this.
  • Supabase (supabase.co) — our backend database. Booking data and authentication are handled here. Your IP is included in server-side requests but is not stored as part of your user profile.

4. How to Delete Your Local Data

You can clear all locally stored GroundsNearMe data at any time:

  • Chrome / Edge: DevTools → Application → Storage → Local Storage → right-click groundsnearme.pk → Clear
  • Firefox: DevTools → Storage → Local Storage → right-click → Delete All
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data → groundsnearme.pk → Remove
  • All browsers: Clear all site data from Settings → Privacy → Clear browsing data

5. Changes to This Policy

If we introduce any new storage mechanisms, this page will be updated. We will not introduce advertising or behavioural tracking without providing clear notice and obtaining your explicit consent first.
